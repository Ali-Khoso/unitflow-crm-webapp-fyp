# Academic references for UnitFlow FYP defence

The four selected works support our **related-research discussion**. They are not proof that UnitFlow's proposed combination has never been built or studied.

## R1 — AI/ML in Oracle Field Service

**Isarapu, R., & Gowda, S. (2024).** "AI and Machine Learning Integration in Oracle Field Service." *International Journal of Computer Trends and Technology*, 72(10), 9–14.

- Research area: machine learning for field-service management, including predictive maintenance and service scheduling.
- DOI: [10.14445/22312803/IJCTT-V72I10P103](https://doi.org/10.14445/22312803/IJCTT-V72I10P103)
- [Publisher article and PDF](https://www.ijcttjournal.org/archives/ijctt-v72i10p103)

## R2 — AI-enabled CRM and competitive advantage

**Yoo, J. W., Park, J., & Park, H. (2024).** "The impact of AI-enabled CRM systems on organizational competitive advantage: A mixed-method approach using BERTopic and PLS-SEM." *Heliyon*, 10(16), e36392.

- Research area: characteristics of AI-enabled CRM and their association with CRM capability and competitive advantage.
- DOI: [10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e36392](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e36392)
- [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39253149/)

## R3 — Systematic review of AI-based CRM

**Ozay, D., Jahanbakht, M., Shoomal, A., & Wang, S. (2024).** "Artificial Intelligence (AI)-based Customer Relationship Management (CRM): a comprehensive bibliometric and systematic literature review with outlook on future research." *Enterprise Information Systems*, 18(7), article 2351869.

- Research area: review of 810 scholarly articles and four themes: data-driven CRM, AI applications, strategic implementation, and AI-enabled customer experience.
- DOI: [10.1080/17517575.2024.2351869](https://doi.org/10.1080/17517575.2024.2351869)
- [Publisher page](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/17517575.2024.2351869)

## R4 — Event-driven financial workflows

**Odofin, O. T., et al. (2024).** "Designing Event-Driven Architecture for Financial Systems Using Kafka, Camunda BPM, and Process Engines." *International Journal of Scientific Research in Science, Engineering and Technology*, 11(4), 427–441.

- Research area: message-driven architecture, process-engine orchestration and reliability, demonstrated in a **financial-systems** context.
- DOI: [10.32628/IJSRSET25121178](https://doi.org/10.32628/IJSRSET25121178)
- [Publisher article](https://ijsrset.com/index.php/home/article/view/IJSRSET25121178)

## Citation and use policy

- The **research focus** above is based on each publisher abstract/metadata (reviewed October 2026); avoid claiming results not established by the study.
- All four are *related* work; a broader literature search is needed to establish the actual prevalence of end-to-end property-aware home-service systems.
- We use "proposed integration and evaluation focus" instead of asserting "no prior research exists."
- The public repository records academic source summaries only and does not contain internal UnitZero documents or proprietary source code.
