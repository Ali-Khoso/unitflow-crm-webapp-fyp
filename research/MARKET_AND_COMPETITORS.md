# Preliminary market and competitor research

**Research stage:** Desk research has been prepared for the proposal defence; customer interviews and firsthand competitive testing remain future work.

## Existing products

| Product | Established general strengths | FYP relevance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GoHighLevel | CRM, marketing and workflow automation | Consider custom CRM objects and automation integration |
| Jobber | Service estimates, scheduling, jobs and customer management | Compare home-service field workflows |
| Housecall Pro | Field-service operations, customer communication and automation | Compare end-to-end service lifecycle and workflow controls |
| Zoho | Large CRM and business-application ecosystem | Study general business SaaS reach and customization |
| ServiceTitan | Broad home/field-service operations | Compare comprehensive commercial platform scope |

**No claim of absent features:** Many of these products already have overlapping CRM, equipment/property history, automation and AI capabilities. This project is not the first to combine service management and automation.

## What is the FYP's defensible focus?

Designing and evaluating a **focused property-aware, multi-tenant service CRM** with a programmable graph-based workflow engine, communication triggers, service-job transitions, auditability, failure handling and limited AI extraction.

This is an **engineering/evaluation focus**, not proof that another commercial product is incapable of the same tasks.
