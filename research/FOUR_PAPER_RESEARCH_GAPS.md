# Paper-by-paper research scope and proposed integration gap

**Interpretation guardrail:** The comparisons below identify a difference in **study focus**, not proof that any capability or study is absent from the worldwide literature.

| Paper | What this selected study examines | What UnitFlow will implement/evaluate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R1 — Isarapu & Gowda (2024)** | AI and ML for field-service operations such as predictive maintenance and scheduling | CRM-to-field-service journey beginning with a customer enquiry, followed by response SLA, estimate, job, invoice and property history |
| **R2 — Yoo et al. (2024)** | AI-enabled CRM capabilities, organizational performance and competitive advantage | Operational workflow integration: human lead response, escalation, technician service and validated AI-to-CRM updates |
| **R3 — Ozay et al. (2024)** | Broad bibliometric/systematic review mapping AI-CRM research themes | An implemented and tested property-aware home-service CRM with AI-assisted extraction and end-to-end execution |
| **R4 — Odofin et al. (2024)** | Kafka, process engines and resilient event-driven orchestration for financial workflows | Custom graph workflow orchestration applied to a multi-tenant service-business process, including delays, retries, idempotency and auditing |

## Proposed combined gap statement for the FYP defence

> Selected studies explore AI-enabled CRM, field-service optimization and event-driven orchestration in different contexts. UnitFlow proposes to integrate and evaluate a property-aware, multi-tenant home-service journey linking lead-response SLAs, customer communications, estimate acceptance, technician jobs, invoices and validated AI-assisted CRM updates.

## What makes this an academic engineering contribution?

1. **Build and integration:** A testable connected business process spanning CRM, property records and service operations.
2. **Workflow engineering:** Model and execute configurable graph workflows with persistent state, delays, branching and replay-safe actions.
3. **Security engineering:** Prevent cross-tenant access and enforce user-role permissions in application services.
4. **AI quality:** Validate and measure structured-information extraction instead of treating AI suggestions as automatically correct.
5. **Evaluation:** Report measured correctness, latency, duplicate prevention, security test results and usability observations.
