# FYP defence: Related Research & Identified Gap

**Slide placement:** After "Existing Systems & Market Research Gap" and before "Market Opportunity & Industry Validation."

## Suggested single-slide content

### Four selected studies

1. **Isarapu & Gowda (2024)** — AI/ML in field service; predictive service and scheduling. [Open paper](https://www.ijcttjournal.org/archives/ijctt-v72i10p103)
2. **Yoo, Park & Park (2024)** — AI-CRM characteristics and competitive advantage. [Open paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39253149/)
3. **Ozay et al. (2024)** — systematic review of 810 AI-CRM studies. [Open paper](https://doi.org/10.1080/17517575.2024.2351869)
4. **Odofin et al. (2024)** — event-driven Kafka/Camunda orchestration in finance. [Open paper](https://ijsrset.com/index.php/home/article/view/IJSRSET25121178)

### Identified integration and evaluation gap

Selected studies examine field-service AI, AI-CRM and financial workflow orchestration in different settings. **UnitFlow proposes** to connect property history, SLA escalation, accepted estimates, technician jobs, invoices and AI-assisted CRM records, then test the resulting system.

**Evaluation focus:** workflow reliability, tenant isolation, processing latency, extraction accuracy and usability.

## Possible panel follow-ups

**Q: Do these papers prove no one else has integrated these functions?**  
A: No, sir. They are representative studies in related areas. Our academically defensible contribution is the implementation and technical evaluation of a clearly scoped system.

**Q: Which study is most relevant to the engine?**  
A: Odofin et al. for the concepts of asynchronous events and orchestration, although its application domain is financial workflows and our proposed implementation stack differs.

**Q: What is the benchmark?**  
A: Clearly specified integration scenarios and measurable success criteria: correct transitions, response-timer processing, retry/idempotency behavior, tenant data isolation, extraction accuracy and usability. No performance result is claimed yet.
